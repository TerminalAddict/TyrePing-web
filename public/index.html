Ask a question on GitHub ↗
What do I need to use TyrePing?

An Android phone running Android 9 or later, Bluetooth Low Energy, and compatible BLE tyre-pressure sensors. TyrePing reads broadcasts from the sensors; it cannot measure pressure using your phone alone.

Which sensors are supported?

Supported sensor families include Sysgration, Pecham, Bekubee/HRTPMS and Wicarlink/LYTPMS. Compatibility depends on the Bluetooth advertisement format, not just the name on the packaging. Factory TPMS sensors that use other radio protocols are not automatically compatible.

Is TyrePing on the Google Play Store?

Not yet. TyrePing is under active development and is currently installed manually from an APK on GitHub. A Play Store release is planned. The installation guide explains the current process.

Can I monitor more than one vehicle?

Yes. Create separate vehicles and assign sensors to their tyres. Cars, motorcycles, trailers and three-wheelers are supported. Automatic ride monitoring watches one selected vehicle at a time.

Does it work in the background?

You can start background monitoring manually, or configure automatic ride monitoring using a paired Bluetooth accessory or a BLE beacon. Bluetooth, notification permissions and Android background restrictions affect operation. Real-world accessory, beacon and screen-off validation is still in progress.

Does it replace manual pressure checks?

Keep using a calibrated gauge and inspecting your tyres regularly. TyrePing provides advisory readings and alerts, and depends on compatible sensors, Bluetooth reception and Android permissions. Experimental pressure-loss detection is off by default and has not yet been validated on a real ride.
