Ready before you ride

The Ready-to-Ride dashboard opens a per-wheel checklist for reading freshness, pressure limits and battery warnings. Sleeping or unseen sensors stay unconfirmed.

Notice a missing sensor

Configurable warnings track sensors that have reported during the session. Parked mode pauses missing-sensor warnings, and Bluetooth recovery gets a grace period.

Save your pressure profiles

Keep named front/rear alert limits for Solo, Pillion, Touring, Towing or your own setup. Applying a profile updates both axles’ limits together.

Switch vehicles your way

Map paired Bluetooth accessories to vehicles, keep manual control when needed, or tap an NFC vehicle tag. The selector can flag warnings on other bound vehicles.

Readings on your home screen

A resizable widget shows cached pressures, temperatures, monitoring status and advancing reading ages. The widget itself does not start scanning.

Keep a tyre maintenance record

Track installations, replacements, tread, pressure checks and notes per wheel. Reminders can be due by date or manually entered mileage.

Find and manage sensors

Discover BLE sensors without printed IDs, inspect candidates and confirm each assignment. Move, swap or rotate bindings with a preview.

Calibrate individual sensors

Apply pressure and temperature corrections per sensor. Live displays and alerts use corrected values; recorded raw readings stay unchanged.

History, graphs and exports

Explore pressure/temperature graphs and reading sessions, export CSV, or capture BLE packets and diagnostic JSONL when you choose.

Speech, tones and vibration

Choose optional alert sounds, spoken warnings, vibration and repeat intervals. Silence them temporarily while visual warnings remain. These options start disabled.

Back up your configuration

Export vehicles, bindings, calibration, profiles, reminders and settings. Restore validates the data and saves a safety copy; history and packet captures export separately.

Recovery and spoken diagnostics

An interrupted session can offer a restart when you reopen the app. Optional spoken diagnostics explain waiting, Bluetooth loss and scan failures.

Tow vehicle and trailer together

Save towing combinations and use a combined dashboard while each vehicle keeps its own sensor assignments and pressure limits.

Tyre and reception insights

Explore axle pressure differences, user-confirmed cold baselines and reception statistics. These observations help you inspect readings and sensor behaviour.

Readings that suit your screen

Use high-visibility colours, adjustable readout sizes, optional portrait cards and Android Auto status and tyre lists.

A home for every sensor

Keep assigned sensors and labelled spares in one inventory, with manufacturer notes, battery readings, last-seen times and installation history. Move sensors between wheels and the spare library with confirmation.

Observe sensor health over time

Review daily battery, signal and reception observations over 30, 90 or 365 days. Gaps and trends need context: they describe what was observed and do not predict sensor failure.

See the whole garage

Garage / Fleet brings every vehicle’s last known tyre readings, freshness, warnings and maintenance state into one overview. Open any vehicle’s own dashboard for a closer look.

Keep handbook pressures nearby

Record tyre sizes, load conditions, cold-pressure recommendations and their source. Compare those references with saved pressure profiles; saving a specification does not change alert limits.

Monitoring controls within reach

Use the Quick Settings tile to open monitoring controls, and choose an optional live tyre notification. Check active, waiting, suspended or stopped status alongside reading ages.

Look back at the ride

Review monitoring-session reports with raw pressure changes, maximum temperatures, missing readings and alert counts. Search alert history and acknowledge entries independently of active alarms. No GPS ride tracking is required.

Make room for maintenance

While parked, use timed inflation / maintenance mode to pause expected pressure, leak, imbalance and missing-sensor alerts. Temperature and battery warnings remain enabled; end maintenance mode before riding.

Compare and replace with care

Compare a fresh sensor reading with a trusted pressure gauge before approving an offset. Guided replacement confirms the old and new sensor while preserving earlier readings and sensor identities.

Choose what to keep

Inspect storage usage, choose retention periods, archive older records as JSONL and preview manual cleanup. History is kept by default; archives are separate from configuration backups.

Compatibility, downloads and how the monitoring tools work.

What do I need to use TyrePing?

An Android phone supported by your chosen build, Bluetooth Low Energy and compatible BLE tyre-pressure sensors. Your phone alone cannot measure tyre pressure. NFC hardware is optional and only needed for tag-based vehicle switching.

Where do I find the current release and changes?

Our downloads page lists available installation options and release notes. APK builds are served from tyreping.com with matching checksums. If store installation is available, follow its link from the same page. Check the notes for the build you choose before installing.

Are all features available in the stable build?

Feature availability varies by build. Compare the stable and pre-release notes on our downloads page. Pre-releases are for testing; physical-device validation and longer-term sensor observations may still be pending.

Which sensors are supported?

Selected Sysgration, Pecham, Bekubee/HRTPMS and Wicarlink/LYTPMS advertisement formats are supported. Discovery can inspect unknown devices, but only recognised, verified readings can be assigned. Compatibility depends on the packet format, not just the brand or wheel labels; review sensor guidance and your build’s release notes before buying hardware.

Can I monitor more than one vehicle?

Yes. Keep separate vehicles and sensor bindings, use accessory-based vehicle selection or NFC switching, and save tow-vehicle/trailer combinations. Builds with Garage / Fleet also show an overview of every vehicle. Selecting the displayed vehicle and setting an automatic monitoring trigger are separate actions.

Can TyrePing predict when a sensor will fail?

No. Sensor-health tools summarise observed battery, signal strength and reception gaps. Sleeping sensors, distance and interference affect those observations. They do not predict sensor failure or confirm tyre condition.

How do I update TyrePing?

Download a newer signed APK from our downloads page and install it over the existing signed app. Some builds also offer a manual update checker in About or Settings. Checking for updates does not install them automatically; read the selected build’s notes first.

Does the widget keep monitoring running?

No. The widget shows cached readings, monitoring status and reading ages. It updates while the app or service is active and through Android’s periodic widget refresh. Start monitoring in TyrePing; a widget by itself does not start scanning.

What happens if Android stops monitoring?

Reopening TyrePing after an interrupted session can offer Restart monitoring or Keep stopped, with an Android exit reason where available. The app cannot notify you while Android has killed it. Bluetooth permissions, battery restrictions and manufacturer background limits still affect monitoring.

Does Ready-to-Ride replace manual tyre checks?

No. The checklist summarises reading freshness, limits and sensor battery warnings; sleeping or unseen sensors are unconfirmed. Keep checking tyres with a calibrated gauge and follow manufacturer guidance. Read the release notes for limitations of experimental tools.
