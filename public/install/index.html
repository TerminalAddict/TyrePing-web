Install TyrePing.

Installing an APK? Download your chosen build from our downloads page, read its release notes and follow the manual steps below. For store installation, use the store link shown on the downloads page when available.

Choose a build

Open TyrePing downloads to choose an available build and read its release notes. APKs and their checksums are hosted on tyreping.com.

The notes describe changes, known limits and testing status. Sensor inventory, fleet views and the newer monitoring tools are available in pre-release builds. The sections below explain where to find them when included in your installed build.

Trying a pre-release? Check its notes before installing. Pre-releases are intended for testing and can be older than the stable build, so compare the versions before changing channels.

Before you begin

  • An Android phone with Bluetooth Low Energy. Check your chosen release’s minimum Android version.
  • Compatible BLE tyre-pressure sensors. Your phone alone cannot measure tyre pressure.
  • Your vehicle’s recommended cold tyre pressures, to choose appropriate alert limits.
  • Optional NFC hardware and a writable NDEF tag if you want tag-based vehicle switching.

1. Download the APK

On the downloads page, choose the stable or pre-release APK. Your download comes directly from tyreping.com. Read that build’s release notes before installing.

To check integrity, compare the downloaded APK’s SHA-256 hash with its matching SHA256SUMS.txt file, available beside the download button.

2. Open and install

Open the APK from Downloads or your browser’s download list. If Android asks, enable Allow from this source for the browser or file manager opening it, then return to the installer and choose Install. You can turn that permission off afterwards.

The exact label varies by phone. Managed work devices may prevent manual installation. The signed app uses nz.paulwillard.tyreping; the debug app is separate and has separate data.

3. Allow Bluetooth access

Read the app privacy policy for how permissions, local records, backups and crash reporting work.

Open TyrePing and switch on Bluetooth. Grant the permissions requested by your Android version:

  • Android 12+: Nearby devices access for Bluetooth discovery and connections.
  • Android 9–11: location permission for Bluetooth discovery. Automatic background discovery on Android 10–11 may require “Allow all the time”. TyrePing does not record GPS location.
  • Notifications: allow these when requested for monitoring status and alerts.

4. Add a vehicle and bind sensors

Create a vehicle and choose its layout. Cars, motorcycles, single- and double-axle trailers and three-wheelers are supported. Other layouts include a car with a spare wheel, vans, utes and monowheels. Layout options depend on your installed build.

For a double-axle trailer, the front axle is the one nearest the hitch. Bind front-left/right and rear-left/right sensors accordingly, then set separate limits if needed. A spare wheel and a monowheel need explicit sensor assignments.

Bind using a supported QR code or a favourite sensor. Tap a tyre outline or open Settings → Sensors to enter an ID, identify a candidate, unassign a sensor, or preview moves, swaps and rotations.

Discover sensors without printed IDs

  1. Open Settings → Sensors → Discover sensors and select a wheel.
  2. While parked, start scanning, choose Mark, then fit sensor, and fit one sensor.
  3. Inspect newly seen or changed devices and verify decoded pressure and temperature against that tyre.
  4. Confirm Assign to selected wheel. Discovery never assigns a nearby device automatically.

Scans last up to two minutes and stop when the page is hidden. Unknown devices can be inspected and exported as JSONL but cannot be assigned. Export session packets before closing discovery. This helps investigate unfamiliar sensors; it does not establish new protocol compatibility.

Sensor inventory and health

In builds that include it, open Settings → Sensor inventory and health to review assigned sensors and unassigned spares. Recognised broadcasts add sensors while scanning; you can also add a labelled spare by decimal or hexadecimal ID, with manufacturer details and notes.

Review the current wheel assignment before moving a sensor or returning it to the spare library. Installation snapshots and the sensor’s earlier readings are retained. Assignments imported from an older build have unknown earlier installation dates rather than guessed dates.

Sensor details show daily battery, signal-strength, packet-count and reception-gap observations across 30, 90 or 365 days. Battery units stay as reported. Trends need at least three observed days spanning a week; a known broadcast interval can enable missed-broadcast estimates that exclude scanner downtime. Sleeping sensors and radio conditions still affect gaps. These observations do not predict sensor failure.

Garage / Fleet and handbook specifications

Open Menu → Garage / Fleet for all registered vehicles, last-known tyre conditions, freshness, warnings and maintenance state. Open vehicle selects its individual dashboard manually. The overview reuses existing observations and does not start a separate scanner.

Manufacturer tyre specifications stores handbook or placard sources, tyre sizes, load conditions and cold-pressure recommendations in your selected unit. Compare them with current and saved pressure-profile limits. Saving a reference never applies a profile or changes an alert threshold.

5. Choose how to monitor

Check readings on the vehicle screen or start background monitoring manually. Automatic ride monitoring can start from a paired Bluetooth accessory or a BLE beacon. One selected vehicle can be watched automatically at a time.

Accessory-based vehicle selection changes the displayed vehicle; it is separate from the automatic ride monitoring trigger. Manual and NFC choices hold until you resume automatic selection or the app process restarts.

If Android interrupts a session, reopen TyrePing to review the saved vehicle and mode, any available Android exit reason, and the Restart monitoring or Keep stopped options. The app cannot notify you while Android has killed it. Check Android background activity and battery restrictions if interruptions recur.

Tow vehicle and trailer together

Use Towing combinations above the dashboard, or Settings → Driving dashboards, to save a car, ute or van with a trailer. Each keeps its own sensors, calibration and alert limits. Selecting a combination holds the tow vehicle as a manual choice; Individual view returns to its own dashboard.

Tyre insights, readability and monitoring checks

Tyre insights can compare fresh left/right readings on the same axle using your chosen difference threshold. Imbalance warnings start disabled, require active monitoring for audible delivery and pause while Parked.

Confirm a cold baseline only after the tyres have cooled to ambient temperature. Every expected wheel needs a bound sensor and a fresh positive reading. Later changes are observations rather than hot-pressure targets; comparisons stop after twelve hours, when readings go stale or when a different sensor is fitted.

Sensor reception quality shows signal strength and packet counts. A known broadcast interval enables estimates that exclude scan interruptions, but sleeping sensors and radio conditions still affect results.

Choose High-visibility mode, Readout size or Use reading cards in portrait in reading settings. Monitoring health check / self-test reviews permissions, Bluetooth, scanner/service state, notifications, audio settings and freshness. Its test alert respects your settings and silence controls; confirm sound and vibration delivery yourself.

Quick controls and maintenance mode

Open Settings → Monitoring controls to start or stop monitoring. On builds with the Quick Settings tile, add TyrePing from Settings; the tile opens those controls and shows active, suspended, waiting or stopped status. An optional Live tyre status notification adds pressures, temperatures and reading ages while the service runs.

Settings → Monitoring tools contains timed inflation / maintenance mode and secondary-vehicle alert routing. Maintenance mode lasts 1–60 minutes for the chosen vehicle. It suppresses pressure, leak, imbalance and missing-sensor delivery while retaining temperature and battery warnings. It expires automatically and ends on a phone reboot. Use it while parked and end it before riding.

Secondary vehicles can use all alerts, notifications only, critical alerts only or history only. The monitored vehicle and its selected trailer retain full alert delivery.

Set up your ride tools

  • Ready-to-Ride: open the per-wheel checklist above the vehicle drawing. Recently healthy readings must be fresh, within limits and free of a reported battery warning. Sleeping, unseen or unknown readings are not a confirmation of tyre condition.
  • Pressure profiles: save named front/rear limits under Settings → Ride tools. The editor uses labelled kPa values. Profiles store your chosen limits, not app-generated pressure recommendations.
  • Missing-sensor warnings: choose a timeout from one to sixty minutes, or disable it. Only sensors that have reported in the current session are enrolled. Parked mode pauses missing warnings; pressure and temperature alerts continue.
  • NFC switching: write the current vehicle’s link to a writable NDEF tag. Writing replaces the tag’s contents after confirmation; tap with the phone unlocked to select that vehicle.
  • Home-screen widget: add TyrePing readings through Android’s widget picker. It follows the selected vehicle and shows cached readings and ages. It does not start scanning by itself.
  • Maintenance: record dates, tyre details, tread, pressure checks and manually entered odometer kilometres. Reminders become due by date or entered mileage; Android may delay background reminder checks.

Choose alerts and calibration

TyrePing offers optional speech or tones, vibration, repeat intervals and a ten-minute silence control. Sound and vibration start disabled. Visual warnings remain while sound is silenced. Spoken monitoring diagnostics are a separate option and start disabled too.

Individual sensor calibration adjusts live pressure and temperature using a multiplier and offset. Calibration follows the physical sensor when assignments change. Raw history and packet recordings are not rewritten.

On builds with Compare with pressure gauge, tap an assigned wheel and compare a fresh TPMS reading against a trusted gauge. Applying the suggested pressure offset requires a reading less than two minutes old and your explicit review; it preserves the existing multiplier and temperature calibration.

Replace sensor guides you through confirming the old and new identities and any conflicting assignment. Earlier readings remain attached to the old sensor.

History and exports

Open Pressure / temperature history and CSV to browse graphs and inferred reading sessions. Sessions come from gaps between readings, not GPS ride logs. Graphs and CSV exports contain raw readings; calibration affects the live display and alerts.

Optional BLE diagnostics capture is off after each process restart. It saves delivered packet data for JSONL export. Discovery exports, persistent diagnostic captures and CSV history are separate tools.

Ride reports and alert history

In Settings → Monitoring tools, builds with ride reports record actual background-monitoring sessions, including automatic triggers. A towing combination gets one report per vehicle selected at the start. Review raw start/end pressure, changes, maximum temperatures, missing readings, long gaps and alert counts. Reports do not use GPS.

These monitoring-session reports are separate from graph sessions inferred from reading gaps. Interrupted rides end at their last saved heartbeat, and report summaries survive later raw-history cleanup.

Alert event history records observed conditions while the app is open or monitoring runs. Search by vehicle, wheel, severity or sensor ID. Acknowledging an entry does not silence an active alarm.

Questionable readings and diagnostic evidence

Where available, Questionable sensor readings lists implausible fields, recognised TPMS packets that failed decoding and unexpected ID changes at the same Bluetooth address. Bindings never change automatically. Zero pressure and finite extreme readings continue through ordinary alerts; unreadable packets cannot become usable tyre readings.

Export issue evidence as JSONL when investigating a sensor. Diagnostic exports can include sensor identifiers and raw radio data, so review their contents before sharing. Anonymised structural exports in the sensor compatibility toolkit omit payload bytes; raw packets are needed for decoder investigation.

Storage, retention and archives

Open Settings → Monitoring tools → Storage and retention to inspect usage and choose retention windows. The default keeps everything. Stop monitoring, preview the proposed cleanup, optionally export older records as a JSON Lines archive, then confirm removal. Active events and rides, and the latest readings for assigned sensors, are protected.

Builds with daily sensor-health summaries can archive or remove old summaries separately. Archives are for offline analysis and cannot be imported as configuration backups. Cleanup frees pages for reuse and may not immediately shrink the database file.

Back up or restore configuration

The Backup and restore tools export vehicles, bindings, calibration, pressure profiles, maintenance records, reminders, accessory mappings and settings as JSON. Builds with inventory and manufacturer references also include spare-sensor metadata and handbook specifications. Reading, installation and reception histories stay separate from configuration backups. History and raw packet captures have their own exports.

Import validates the file, shows a confirmation and saves a safety copy. Matching vehicles and bindings are replaced; other vehicles and recorded history remain. Monitoring stops during restore and the app restarts. Re-arm automatic watches yourself; running sessions, permissions, temporary maintenance timers and temporary silence are not transferred.

Updating TyrePing

Download a newer signed APK from the downloads page and install it over the existing signed app. Android normally retains app data when the application ID and signing certificate match.

Current Release builds, including signed pre-release APKs, have no in-app update checker. Install a newer website-hosted APK manually; Google Play handles updates for store installations when available.

TyrePing Debug has About → Check for TyrePing updates (also in Settings). It contacts GitHub only when you choose Check now and can open a signed APK in the browser. It does not install automatically. Those signed APKs install or update the separate signed app, not Debug. Older Release builds may also include this checker. See the app privacy policy for request details.

Debug builds remain separate. Installing an older APK over a newer version may be blocked by Android; check your chosen release’s update instructions before changing builds. ADB sensor simulation is only available in debug builds.

Sensor compatibility

Selected advertisement formats used by Sysgration, Pecham, Bekubee/HRTPMS and Wicarlink/LYTPMS are supported. Compatibility depends on the packet format, not just the brand or wheel labels. Factory TPMS systems using other radio protocols are not automatically compatible with a phone. The MRD-TY-T05-04 kit remains unverified; its wheel labels do not establish a compatible packet format.

Read the current release notes for changes to sensor support. Discovery can inspect unfamiliar broadcasts, but a captured packet alone does not establish compatibility. Verify decoded values; if the format is unknown or values are implausible, export a diagnostic capture for investigation.

If readings do not appear

  • Check Bluetooth, requested permissions, sensor battery and proximity.
  • Confirm sensor compatibility and the assignment to the correct vehicle and wheel.
  • Check reading timestamps rather than treating cached values as fresh.
  • If background monitoring stops, check Android battery/background restrictions and reopen TyrePing to review recovery information.

Review the release notes for your build for known limitations and testing information. Keep a record of your Android version, sensor model and observed behaviour when investigating a problem.

Keep your normal tyre checks. TyrePing is advisory. Use a calibrated gauge and follow manufacturer guidance. Ready-to-Ride is an information check; read the release notes before relying on experimental features.
