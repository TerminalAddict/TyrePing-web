Let’s get you connected

Install TyrePing.

Installing an APK? Download your chosen build from our downloads page, read its release notes and follow the manual steps below. For store installation, use the store link shown on the downloads page when available.

Choose a build

Open TyrePing downloads to choose an available build and read its release notes. APKs and their checksums are hosted on tyreping.com.

The notes describe changes, known limits and testing status. Feature availability can differ between builds; the instructions below introduce the main workflows.

Trying a pre-release? Check its notes before installing. Pre-releases are intended for testing and can be older than the stable build, so compare the versions before changing channels.

Before you begin

  • An Android phone with Bluetooth Low Energy. Check your chosen release’s minimum Android version.
  • Compatible BLE tyre-pressure sensors. Your phone alone cannot measure tyre pressure.
  • Your vehicle’s recommended cold tyre pressures, to choose appropriate alert limits.
  • Optional NFC hardware and a writable NDEF tag if you want tag-based vehicle switching.

1. Download the APK

On the downloads page, choose the stable or pre-release APK. Your download comes directly from tyreping.com. Read that build’s release notes before installing.

To check integrity, compare the downloaded APK’s SHA-256 hash with its matching SHA256SUMS.txt file, available beside the download button.

2. Open and install

Open the APK from Downloads or your browser’s download list. If Android asks, enable Allow from this source for the browser or file manager opening it, then return to the installer and choose Install. You can turn that permission off afterwards.

The exact label varies by phone. Managed work devices may prevent manual installation. The signed app uses nz.paulwillard.tyreping; the debug app is separate and has separate data.

3. Allow Bluetooth access

Open TyrePing and switch on Bluetooth. Grant the permissions requested by your Android version:

  • Android 12+: Nearby devices access for Bluetooth discovery and connections.
  • Android 9–11: location permission for Bluetooth discovery. Automatic background discovery on Android 10–11 may require “Allow all the time”. TyrePing does not record GPS location.
  • Notifications: allow these when requested for monitoring status and alerts.

4. Add a vehicle and bind sensors

Create a vehicle and choose its layout. Cars, motorcycles, single- and double-axle trailers and three-wheelers are supported. Other layouts include a car with a spare wheel, vans, utes and monowheels. Layout options depend on your installed build.

For a double-axle trailer, the front axle is the one nearest the hitch. Bind front-left/right and rear-left/right sensors accordingly, then set separate limits if needed. A spare wheel and a monowheel need explicit sensor assignments.

Bind using a supported QR code or a favourite sensor. Tap a tyre outline or open Settings → Sensors to enter an ID, identify a candidate, unassign a sensor, or preview moves, swaps and rotations.

Discover sensors without printed IDs

  1. Open Settings → Sensors → Discover sensors and select a wheel.
  2. While parked, start scanning, choose Mark, then fit sensor, and fit one sensor.
  3. Inspect newly seen or changed devices and verify decoded pressure and temperature against that tyre.
  4. Confirm Assign to selected wheel. Discovery never assigns a nearby device automatically.

Scans last up to two minutes and stop when the page is hidden. Unknown devices can be inspected and exported as JSONL but cannot be assigned. Export session packets before closing discovery. This helps investigate unfamiliar sensors; it does not establish new protocol compatibility.

5. Choose how to monitor

Check readings on the vehicle screen or start background monitoring manually. Automatic ride monitoring can start from a paired Bluetooth accessory or a BLE beacon. One selected vehicle can be watched automatically at a time.

Accessory-based vehicle selection changes the displayed vehicle; it is separate from the automatic ride monitoring trigger. Manual and NFC choices hold until you resume automatic selection or the app process restarts.

If Android interrupts a session, reopen TyrePing to review the saved vehicle and mode, any available Android exit reason, and the Restart monitoring or Keep stopped options. The app cannot notify you while Android has killed it. Check Android background activity and battery restrictions if interruptions recur.

Set up your ride tools

  • Ready-to-Ride: open the per-wheel checklist above the vehicle drawing. Recently healthy readings must be fresh, within limits and free of a reported battery warning. Sleeping, unseen or unknown readings are not a confirmation of tyre condition.
  • Pressure profiles: save named front/rear limits under Settings → Ride tools. The editor uses labelled kPa values. Profiles store your chosen limits, not app-generated pressure recommendations.
  • Missing-sensor warnings: choose a timeout from one to sixty minutes, or disable it. Only sensors that have reported in the current session are enrolled. Parked mode pauses missing warnings; pressure and temperature alerts continue.
  • NFC switching: write the current vehicle’s link to a writable NDEF tag. Writing replaces the tag’s contents after confirmation; tap with the phone unlocked to select that vehicle.
  • Home-screen widget: add TyrePing readings through Android’s widget picker. It follows the selected vehicle and shows cached readings and ages. It does not start scanning by itself.
  • Maintenance: record dates, tyre details, tread, pressure checks and manually entered odometer kilometres. Reminders become due by date or entered mileage; Android may delay background reminder checks.

Choose alerts and calibration

TyrePing offers optional speech or tones, vibration, repeat intervals and a ten-minute silence control. Sound and vibration start disabled. Visual warnings remain while sound is silenced. Spoken monitoring diagnostics are a separate option and start disabled too.

Individual sensor calibration adjusts live pressure and temperature using a multiplier and offset. Calibration follows the physical sensor when assignments change. Raw history and packet recordings are not rewritten.

History and exports

Open Pressure / temperature history and CSV to browse graphs and inferred reading sessions. Sessions come from gaps between readings, not GPS ride logs. Graphs and CSV exports contain raw readings; calibration affects the live display and alerts.

Optional BLE diagnostics capture is off after each process restart. It saves delivered packet data for JSONL export. Discovery exports, persistent diagnostic captures and CSV history are separate tools.

Back up or restore configuration

The Backup and restore tools export vehicles, bindings, calibration, pressure profiles, maintenance records, reminders, accessory mappings and settings as JSON. History and raw packet captures have separate exports.

Import validates the file, shows a confirmation and saves a safety copy. Matching vehicles and bindings are replaced; other vehicles and recorded history remain. Monitoring stops during restore and the app restarts. Re-arm automatic watches yourself; running sessions, permissions and temporary silence are not transferred.

Updating TyrePing

Download a newer signed APK from the downloads page and install it over the existing signed app. Android normally retains app data when the application ID and signing certificate match.

Debug builds remain separate. Installing an older APK over a newer version may be blocked by Android; check your chosen release’s update instructions before changing builds. ADB sensor simulation is only available in debug builds.

Sensor compatibility

Selected advertisement formats used by Sysgration, Pecham, Bekubee/HRTPMS and Wicarlink/LYTPMS are supported. Compatibility depends on the packet format, not just the brand or wheel labels. Factory TPMS systems using other radio protocols are not automatically compatible with a phone.

Check the current project documentation for sensor support. Discovery can inspect unfamiliar broadcasts, but a captured packet alone does not establish compatibility. Verify decoded values; if the format is unknown or values are implausible, export a diagnostic capture for investigation.

If readings do not appear

  • Check Bluetooth, requested permissions, sensor battery and proximity.
  • Confirm sensor compatibility and the assignment to the correct vehicle and wheel.
  • Check reading timestamps rather than treating cached values as fresh.
  • If background monitoring stops, check Android battery/background restrictions and reopen TyrePing to review recovery information.

Use the project testing checklist or open a GitHub issue with your Android version, sensor model and observed behaviour.

Keep your normal tyre checks. TyrePing is advisory. Use a calibrated gauge and follow manufacturer guidance. Ready-to-Ride is an information check; read the release notes before relying on experimental features.
